# App Store Listing Assets Guide for Kwaaiman Car Services

## Store Listing Requirements

### 1. App Title
- **Recommended:** "Kwaaiman - Premium Ride Service"
- **Max Length:** 30 characters (Google Play) / 50 characters (Apple App Store)

### 2. Short Description
- **Google Play:** Max 80 characters
- **Example:** "Experience premium rides with Kwaaiman. Reliable, safe, and affordable transportation in South Africa."

### 3. Full Description
- **Google Play:** Max 4000 characters
- **Template:**
```
Discover the future of ride-hailing with Kwaaiman - South Africa's premier transportation platform designed for your convenience and safety.

 FEATURES:
• Book rides instantly with real-time tracking
• Multiple service options: Standard, Premium, Delivery
• Cashless payments with various payment methods
• Professional, vetted drivers
• 24/7 customer support
• Safety features including emergency contacts
• Driver registration for earning opportunities

 PREMIUM EXPERIENCE:
• Zero commission rides for premium members
• Luxury vehicle options
• Priority booking system
• Enhanced amenities: water, phone chargers, snacks

 SOUTH AFRICAN FOCUS:
• Built specifically for SA conditions
• Local customer support
• Township connectivity
• Multilingual support

 SAFETY & SECURITY:
• Biometric authentication
• Trip sharing with family/friends
• Real-time location sharing
• Emergency SOS feature

Join thousands of satisfied customers who trust Kwaaiman for their transportation needs. Download now and experience the difference!
```

### 4. Screenshots Requirements
- **Google Play:** 2-8 screenshots (minimum 2)
- **Required dimensions:** 1024 x 500 pixels (recommended)
- **Accepted formats:** JPG or PNG
- **Max size:** 8MB per image

#### Suggested Screenshots:
1. Splash screen with brand logo
2. Main booking interface with map
3. Ride selection options (economy, comfort, premium)
4. Real-time tracking screen
5. Payment methods screen
6. Profile/trip history screen
7. Driver dashboard (if applicable)

### 5. Feature Graphic
- **Dimensions:** 1024 x 500 pixels
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Max size:** 2MB
- **Purpose:** Appears below the app title on the Play Store page

### 6. Promo Video (Optional)
- **Format:** MP4
- **Max size:** 2GB
- **Max duration:** 30 seconds
- **Resolution:** 720p or higher

### 7. App Store Categories
- **Primary:** Travel & Local
- **Secondary:** Utilities (optional)

### 8. Privacy Policy URL
You need to provide a privacy policy URL that explains how user data is collected, used, and protected.

### 9. Contact Information
- **Developer Email:** Your support email
- **Developer Website:** Your company website
- **Support URL:** Help and support page

### 10. Content Rating
- Self-certify based on app content
- For ride-hailing app: Usually rated for ages 3+

### 11. App Store Keywords

- taxi

- bolt

### 12. Recent Changes (What's New)
- For initial release: "Initial release of Kwaaiman Car Services"
- For updates: Detail specific improvements and new features

## Pre-Launch Checklist

- [ ] App icon created and implemented

- [ ] All permissions justified and documented
